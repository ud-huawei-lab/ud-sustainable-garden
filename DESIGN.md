# Garden interface

Authentication preserves the original dashboard's centered, understated branded sign-in panel, visible field labels, password-manager autocomplete, neutral submit button, inline errors, and existing light/dark theme. Authenticated navigation includes a labeled logout control. No dashboard data mounts before the server validates the session.

Operate mode: current conditions, individual sensor history, live cameras, and site exploration.

Preserve the user's weather-dashboard reference: University of Dubai branding, fine navigation, large light-weight readings, two top panels and three lower panels on Overview. Home adds the Three.js site view. Every navigation item renders its own page, rather than scrolling to a section.

White / black page backgrounds with an experimental subtle green radial glow fading from the upper right; neutral surfaces and cards; Inter with native sans-serif fallback; 12–18px radii on cards. Home has no outer frame. Values are placed in a collapsible Environment panel. No card tints: color is reserved for UV, AQI and irradiance scales with a thin current-value marker. Irradiance bands describe intensity, not a safety classification. All station readings are grouped by measurement type, retaining individual sensor histories.

Reading combines all current values and history in three independent category columns, adapting to two and then one on smaller screens. Every sensor keeps a category icon, label, current value, and history action. Chart dialogs use native modal focus management and Escape dismissal. Numeric charts retain gaps; directional data uses scatter; rainfall uses bars; categorical values use a table. A value tooltip follows the pointer and stays within chart edges. There is no chart slider; focusing the graph enables arrow-key, Home, and End navigation with a live text readout.

The 3D scene uses Three.js geometry and lighting. The user's sketch is the layout authority: rear ground-mounted solar panels on the left, two extractors in the middle, two pots to their right, tank behind the pots. A separate full-length solar parking canopy stands in front. Sketch numbers denote ten parking spaces, independent of the solar panel count. Camera 1 mounts on an extractor; Camera 2 near the middle of the canopy structure. No freestanding camera poles. Tubes connect extractors → tank → pots, with explicitly illustrative directional animation. No invented water level or plant sensor. Orbit, zoom, and reset have explicit buttons.

No status badges or dots. AI uses a bottom composer and fixed-height reply panel, never a modal. Model selection and file size remain in the composer; a colored circular context gauge expands to show actual input/output token counts and the 32,768-token window. Voice controls are omitted. Replies follow the bottom unless the reader scrolls up. Missing or failed data is explained in plain text. Mobile layouts stack the weather panels and use two sensor columns, with a fitted 3D view and centered scrollable history dialog.

CCTV uses clean edge-to-edge feeds, subtle camera name/time/fullscreen overlays, skeleton startup, and a centered retry state. Compact hover/focus playback controls provide play/pause, mute, volume, and return-to-live; touch controls stay visible. No native transport controls, duplicate expanded player, or snapshot request during startup. Reading has no promotional introduction or duplicate View history action; each tile keeps one top-right arrow. Data, history, session, and lazy scene loading use skeletons with reduced-motion support. Home offers 3D and an orthographic 2D plan, with gentle revolving and labels toggles in a compact settings menu. Labels start hidden. Revolving is available in 3D only.

Home hides the approximation note and footer text. Motion buttons and display settings share the top-right toolbar. The camera assistant performs user-triggered analysis of three recent frames; it is not a continuous video watcher. The model list shows installed vision models only, including file size and model maximum context.

The chat composer and reply panel use neutral frosted glass. The compact model selector, camera selector, circular context control, and send button are grouped on the right. Focus borders are absent in the chat; keyboard-focused controls use a subtle background highlight. The reply area stays 320px high (capped by the viewport), auto-follows new messages, and preserves scrollback when the reader moves up.

The composer is a single compact row. Model selection uses an anchored glass picker instead of native form chrome. Enter sends; Shift+Enter inserts a newline, and IME composition is preserved.

Live camera controls use a dark glass dock on the right above the name/time caption: pause/play, sound, a volume popover, Live, and fullscreen. PTZ opens as a compact overlay on the video without shrinking, shifting, or cropping it. The feed remains clear on desktop until hover or keyboard focus; paused feeds and touch screens keep controls visible. Pan/tilt uses short deliberate nudges and an explicit Stop; no calibration, continuous hold, or automated AI movement is offered.

The sandy slope is behind the collection tank, beyond the rear edge of the pavement. Mango and Lemon stay in the two pots. Ghaf is on the upper slope; Sidr is farther behind it and lower, with staggered spacing. Irrigation runs behind the tank and follows the descending terrain. These are illustrative elevations, not surveyed dimensions. Each tree has its own label under the existing Labels toggle. Camera direction opens a compact glass overlay at the top-left of the full-size video; playback and the independent volume popover stay on the right.

Garden settings include a Sun lighting switch, off by default. The default view has steady, bright neutral lighting. Enabling the switch offers live sensor-based lighting and a play/pause sun time-lapse, an illustrative 90-second day cycle with a clock and Return to live. There is no visible sun sphere. Live lighting uses Home Assistant elevation and azimuth for direction and illuminance for intensity, falling back to irradiance, then sun elevation when measurements are missing. Intensity is bounded and gently smoothed, with readable ambient fill. The day cycle retains simulated lighting. Disabling Sun lighting stops the cycle and restores steady lighting. The model is not compass-surveyed, so shadows are illustrative. Tree sway responds to normalized live wind speed and direction with bounded strength and smooth transitions; pots remain stationary. Missing wind stops sway, and reduced-motion preferences suppress it.
